# Executive Summary

## Business objective

Create a reliable analytical platform for a Swiss automotive quotation marketplace so management can monitor demand, conversion, quote competitiveness, dealer service, channel quality, vehicle value, and data operations from one governed model.

## Key findings

- The curated dataset contains 99,813 inquiries, 343,120 valid dealer quotes, and 32,464 conversions. Inquiry conversion is 32.52%, average accepted value is CHF 21.4k, and total accepted value is CHF 695.1m.
- Inquiry volume grew 32.1% from 2022 to 2025, while annual conversion remained close to 32%; growth is volume-led rather than efficiency-led.
- First quote response under eight hours is associated with 33.27% conversion versus 29.43% at eight hours or more; the annual gap remains positive in all four years.
- Dealer Referral converts at 38.43%, 11.62 percentage points above Social Media and ahead in each year; channel volume should therefore be evaluated with quality and, when available, cost.
- Accepted quotes are closer to appraised value and faster than non-accepted quotes. Dealer win rates show wide, volume-supported dispersion.
- SUVs account for 51.45% of accepted value. Older vehicles have 2.03 times the relative quote dispersion of 0–2-year vehicles.

## Commercial risks and opportunities

The largest addressable risks are slow first responses, low-competitiveness dealers, weak paid/social lead quality, and pricing exceptions hidden by aggregate averages. Opportunities include an eight-hour response SLA, dealer scorecards, referral-channel protection, SUV coverage, and relative-spread controls for older vehicles.

## Recommendations

1. Implement an eight-hour first-response SLA with dealer alerts and inquiry-volume guardrails.
2. Use a dealer scorecard combining win rate, competitiveness, response, accepted value, and quote volume.
3. Add acquisition cost to channel reporting before budget reallocation; protect referral capacity immediately.
4. Review the 1,162 soft quote anomalies and track issue patterns by dealer, brand, and vehicle age.
5. Keep data-quality metrics and pipeline counts in the operating dashboard; the transformation corrected or quarantined issues that would otherwise overstate outcomes.

*The dataset is synthetic and reproducible. Findings demonstrate the analytical solution and are not claims about a real company or market.*
